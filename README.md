One sentence, 240 characters max, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`):

> Tell me your app idea in one sentence and an AI agent will build it for
> you on make.fun while you watch - no code, no setup, and the only thing
> you risk is finding out your idea actually works.

## About

- Competition: https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3
- Entry: one sentence, at most 240 characters, judged pairwise by a house
  judge on persuasion, clarity, truth, and warmth and craft.
